# Material Symbols Rounded

Google Material Design Icons, Apache License 2.0. License bundled by core:designsystem in `src/main/res/raw/material_symbols_license.txt`.

Category icons: calendar_month, sensors, star, movie, check_circle, auto_awesome, and chevron_right. Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/symbols/web (retrieved 2026-09-21), each corresponding `materialsymbolsrounded/<name>_24px.svg`. Geometry retained, with the SVG viewport translated for Android vectors and fill normalized to black for Compose tinting.

The Anime/Manga selector reuses the tv and menu_book geometry documented in `feature/you/impl/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
